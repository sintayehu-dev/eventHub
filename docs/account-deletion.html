🗑️ Account Deletion - Ethio Events

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

How to Delete Your Account

In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the Ethio Events app on your device
  2. Navigate to the Profile section
  3. Tap the delete icon (trash can) on the right side of your profile card
  4. Read the warning message carefully
  5. Tap "Delete Forever" to confirm
  6. Your account will be deleted immediately

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • ✓ Your profile and personal information
  • ✓ All events you created
  • ✓ Your event registrations and tickets
  • ✓ All uploaded photos and media files
  • ✓ Your staff assignments
  • ✓ Authentication credentials
Note: This action cannot be undone. Make sure to download any important data before deleting your account.

Alternative: Request Deletion via Email

If you're unable to delete your account through the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us:

Contact Information

Email: support@ethioevents.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your full name
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email once your account has been deleted.

Data Retention

After account deletion:

  • Your data is immediately removed from active systems
  • Backup data may be retained for up to 90 days for recovery purposes
  • After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted
  • Some data may be retained if required by law
