## If Reviewers Cannot Access Your App:

Google Play will reject your submission. Make sure:
1. Test credentials are correct and working
2. No additional verification steps are required
3. Account has access to all features
4. Credentials are in English
5. No location-based restrictions
6. No time-limited passwords or OTPs
